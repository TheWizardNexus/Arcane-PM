Docs / Task records
Proposed record contents

Keep the useful context with the assignment.

A task record should explain the work without making its next owner reconstruct an entire conversation.

What a record carries

DetailPurpose
AssignmentThe concrete outcome being requested.
Project and destinationWhere the work belongs and where a prepared handoff should go.
Working stateWhat preparation or execution is actually underway.
DecisionsChoices already made and the basis for them.
Open questionsFacts or consequential choices still unresolved.
Selected sourcesComplete original material included for the next owner.
Prepared resultThe research, draft, or other work ready to use.
Delivery stateWhether the destination has actually accepted the handoff.

Keep originals complete

A short brief explains why a source matters. The selected original remains available in full and unchanged, with source metadata and commentary stored separately. A concise task view leads to that full material instead of replacing it.

These details describe the application’s proposed domain model. They are explanatory labels, rather than serialized field names or a callable API.
